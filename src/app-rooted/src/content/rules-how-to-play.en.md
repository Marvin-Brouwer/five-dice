## How to play

The game consists of 13 rounds. Each round allows for up to 3 rolls. On the first roll you must roll all the dice; on rolls 2 and 3 you may choose which dice to re-roll. Re-rolling is optional — if you are happy with your score on an earlier roll, you may apply it immediately.
