## About the game

Five dice is a turn-based dice game that can be played anywhere. The objective is to score the highest number of points possible by rolling certain combinations with the five dice. It is typically played by multiple players but you *can* play it by yourself.

## Rounds and rolls

The game consists of 13 rounds. Each round allows for up to 3 rolls. On the first roll you must roll all the dice; on rolls 2 and 3 you may choose which dice to re-roll. Re-rolling is optional. If you are happy with your score on an earlier roll, you may apply it immediately.
